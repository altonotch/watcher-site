Common questions

What's free, and what does Pro add?

Timers, the menu bar, the last 30 days of history and Toggl Sync Now are free, with as many timers running at once as you like. Pro adds automatic Toggl sync, changing timers from the command line and the /watcher skill, and history older than 30 days.

How does the trial work?

Start the free 14-day trial from Watcher to try every Pro feature. When it ends, Pro features lock until you buy Watcher Pro once. Your timers and history are kept either way.

I bought Pro on another Mac. How do I get it here?

Sign in to the Mac App Store with the same Apple Account, then choose Restore Purchases in Watcher.

How do I connect Toggl?

Open Watcher's settings, go to Toggl, and pick the one you use at the top:

  • Toggl (the new Toggl at focus.toggl.com): copy your API key from Toggl settings. Then open Toggl in your browser and copy the address from the address bar, which tells Watcher your organization. Paste both in Watcher and click Connect.
  • Toggl Track: copy your API token from your Toggl Track profile, paste it in Watcher and click Connect.

You can then pick a default project. Watcher syncs to one of the two at a time.

How do I use Watcher from Terminal or a coding agent?

Open Watcher's settings, go to Command Line, and install the watcher command and the skill for Claude Code or Codex. Run watcher help to see the commands.

Where is my data?

On your Mac only. See the privacy policy for what Toggl sync sends.
