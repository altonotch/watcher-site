Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

Watcher doesn't collect any data about you. It has no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking. Your timers stay on your Mac.

What stays on your Mac

  • Your timers and their history are stored in Watcher's container on your Mac. They aren't sent anywhere unless you turn on Toggl sync.
  • Settings, including a Toggl API token if you add one, are kept in Watcher's preferences on your Mac.

Toggl sync (optional)

If you connect a Toggl Track account, Watcher talks only to Toggl's API (api.track.toggl.com), over HTTPS, using your API token. It sends each timer's name, start and stop times, duration, and the workspace and project you picked. It also reads your Toggl profile, projects and recent time entries so the two stay in step. Data held in Toggl falls under Toggl's privacy policy. Nothing goes to Toggl unless you connect an account.

Purchases

The trial and Watcher Pro are bought through the Mac App Store. Apple handles payment. Watcher only learns whether you've started the trial or own Pro, never your payment details.

Command line and coding agents (optional)

The watcher command and the /watcher skill run on your Mac. If you let a coding agent such as Claude Code or Codex use them, the agent sees what the command prints, such as timer names and times. That agent's own terms cover what it does with that output.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in macOS settings, Apple may pass on anonymous crash reports. Watcher sends nothing itself.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and its date will be updated.
